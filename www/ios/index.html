Email for an invitation

Opens your email app. Send the message to request an invitation when testing opens; this does not enroll you automatically.

What you’ll need

An iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Transcription runs on your device after an English speech-model download of about 500 MB. A configured Field Theory Mac and your coding-agent tools are needed for agent handoff.

Once your invitation is ready

  1. Install Apple’s TestFlight app on your device.
  2. Open your invitation on that device and install Field Theory.
  3. Try recording, editing, stacking instructions, and handing work to your Mac. Tell us what feels useful, confusing, slow, or broken.

Send feedback from Field Theory’s page in TestFlight, or share a screenshot through TestFlight. Include the steps that led to a problem and what you expected to happen.
