In electronics, when you flip a switch, the circuit doesn't instantly reach its new state. There's a transient period before the signal stabilizes. Engineers call this the settling time.

File watchers have the same problem. Our reconciliation scan runs at exactly this boundary: one final sweep to catch anything that slipped through during initialization.
