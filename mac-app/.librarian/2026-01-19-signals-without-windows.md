In 1887, Heinrich Hertz proved electromagnetic waves existed by building a spark gap transmitter. He could see sparks jump in his receiver across the room—proof the signal arrived. But he had no way to see the wave itself traveling through space. The evidence was indirect: transmitter fires, receiver responds, something must have crossed the gap.

Debugging distributed systems has the same shape. A sound plays—the signal arrived somewhere. A window doesn't appear—something between event and render failed silently. The hard part is never knowing if the signal was sent; it's knowing which receiver didn't fire and why.
